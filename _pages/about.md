I am a **Software Development Engineer II** on the **AWS Network Core** team at Amazon Web Services in Santa Clara, California. My work centers on distributed systems, large-scale cloud network infrastructure, and SDN routing protocols.

Prior to AWS, I completed my Master of Science in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of California, Irvine, and my Bachelor of Science in Computing at Macao Polytechnic University.

### Research & Interests
- **Cloud & Network Infrastructure:** Distributed routing engines, Software-Defined Networking (SDN), high-concurrency systems (Rust/Tokio), and fault-tolerant network fabrics.
- **Machine Learning & Systems:** Efficient model evaluation, layerwise quantization, and time series forecasting.
